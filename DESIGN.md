# gmotion interface direction

An Indonesian video workspace: write a brief, edit the draft, preview, revise, export.
People use the editor beside a playing video; dark, low-chroma green surfaces keep
attention on the footage and reduce the change in brightness around the canvas.

Retain the existing emerald identity. Use emerald for the primary action, selected
controls, and completion. Keep inactive icons neutral. Red and amber mean errors
and warnings. Background #101612, panel #19221d, raised #28352d, border #35443b,
body #e9eeeb, secondary #9aa99f, primary action #6ee7b7 with #101612 text.

Geist is the existing product family; use it throughout for legible Indonesian
form labels and editing. Mono is reserved for code, time, and numeric data.
Sentence case, 14px body minimum in main forms, 6–12px corners, no decorative
shadows or gradients. Native controls and clear keyboard focus are essential.

The home page is a working brief, not a marketing landing page. A compact left
introduction orients the user; the wider right column holds the task. Starter
prompts are an indexed reading list, projects are navigable rows. Sequence numbers
belong only to the actual brief → draft → video workflow and ordered scenes.

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Identity comes from the wordmark, restrained green
surfaces, editing vocabulary, and scene-based composition. Motion communicates
loading and selection; reduced motion is honored. No invented assets or metrics.
