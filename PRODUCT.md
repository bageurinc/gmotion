# GMotion

## Register

product

## Users

People creating narrated explainer videos through an Indonesian-language SaaS.
They supply a topic, review the narration, preview, revise, and export a video.

## Product Purpose

Turn an approved story into an understandable video. Reusable SVG assets are an
additional building block: their parts and actions must explain the narration.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, restrained. The existing interface direction is in DESIGN.md.

## Anti-references

Avoid unrecognizable objects assembled from generic blocks and camera-only motion
that does not demonstrate the process. Do not present illustrative mechanisms as
accurate engineering or anatomy.

## Design Principles

- Make the subject recognizable at phone-video size.
- Keep object shape and projection consistent between scenes.
- Use named parts and explicit state to make assets reusable.
- Make motion demonstrate the spoken action.
- Begin with a complete example before expanding the asset library.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep explanatory text legible and controls keyboard accessible. Honor reduced
motion in interactive previews; exported video animation follows its timeline.
